Good to know

Questions, answered

How long does a custom outfit take?

It depends on the style and how busy we are. Message us with your event date and we will confirm a timeline when you place the order.

Can I send my own fabric or a photo of a style?

Yes. Send a photo of the style you like on WhatsApp, and bring your own fabric or ask us to source it.

What does pay on delivery mean?

You do not pay online. You order on WhatsApp, we deliver, and you pay the rider or us when the Stem Supplements reach you.

Are Stem Supplements a medicine?

No. Stem Supplements is a food and dietary supplement. It does not replace a balanced diet or medical advice. If you are pregnant, nursing, on medication or have a health condition, speak to your doctor or pharmacist first.
